# Centrifugal Pump Model CP-2000 — Maintenance Manual

Document ID: MM-CP2000-EN
Revision: 3.1
Applies to: CP-2000 series horizontal end-suction centrifugal pumps

## 1. Overview

The CP-2000 is a single-stage, horizontal, end-suction centrifugal pump designed
for clean and lightly contaminated water transfer in industrial plants. This
manual covers routine maintenance, lubrication, inspection intervals, and
troubleshooting for maintenance technicians. Read the safety section before
performing any work on the unit.

## 2. Technical Specifications

- Rated flow: 120 m3/h
- Rated head: 45 m
- Nominal speed: 2950 rpm
- Motor power: 22 kW
- Maximum working pressure: 16 bar
- Maximum liquid temperature: 90 °C
- Suction connection: DN100 (4 in) flange
- Discharge connection: DN80 (3 in) flange
- Bearing type: deep-groove ball bearings, 6309 (drive end) and 6207 (non-drive end)
- Mechanical seal: single unbalanced cartridge seal, type MS-32
- Net weight (bare pump): 145 kg

## 3. Safety Precautions

Always follow the plant lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedure before any maintenance.
De-energize the motor at the local disconnect and verify zero energy before
opening the pump. Close the suction and discharge isolation valves and relieve
the internal pressure through the drain plug before disassembly. Allow the pump
casing to cool below 40 °C when it has been handling hot liquid. Never operate
the pump dry, as this destroys the mechanical seal within seconds. Wear eye
protection and chemical-resistant gloves when draining the casing.

## 4. Routine Maintenance Schedule

The following intervals assume continuous duty in a clean-water application.
Shorten the intervals by half for abrasive or high-temperature service.

- Daily: check discharge pressure and note any abnormal noise or vibration.
- Weekly: inspect the mechanical seal area for leakage; a few drops per minute
  is acceptable during run-in, continuous streaming is not.
- Monthly: measure bearing housing temperature; it must not exceed 80 °C or
  ambient + 40 °C, whichever is lower.
- Every 4000 operating hours: regrease the bearings (see Section 5).
- Every 8000 operating hours: replace the mechanical seal cartridge (type MS-32).
- Every 16000 operating hours or 3 years: replace both bearings and perform a
  full alignment check.

## 5. Lubrication

The CP-2000 uses grease-lubricated bearings. Use a lithium-complex grease with
NLGI grade 2 and a base oil viscosity of 100 cSt at 40 °C. Do not mix greases
of different thickener types. Inject 15 grams of grease into the drive-end
bearing and 10 grams into the non-drive-end bearing at each 4000-hour interval.
Over-greasing causes the bearing to overheat; wipe away any excess that purges
from the relief port. Rotate the shaft by hand while greasing to distribute the
lubricant evenly.

## 6. Bearing Replacement Procedure

1. Follow the LOTO and depressurization steps in Section 3.
2. Remove the coupling guard and decouple the pump from the motor.
3. Remove the bearing housing end cover.
4. Use a bearing puller to remove the drive-end (6309) and non-drive-end (6207)
   bearings from the shaft. Never strike the bearing directly with a hammer.
5. Clean the shaft seats and inspect for scoring; the shaft diameter tolerance
   at the bearing seat is 45 mm h5 (drive end).
6. Heat the new bearings to a maximum of 110 °C in an induction heater and slide
   them onto the shaft. Do not exceed 120 °C or the bearing hardness degrades.
7. Reassemble in reverse order and torque the bearing housing bolts to 45 Nm.

## 7. Mechanical Seal Replacement

The MS-32 is a cartridge seal, so it is replaced as a complete unit. Do not
disassemble the cartridge. Remove the four set screws on the drive collar,
withdraw the old cartridge, clean the seal chamber, and slide the new cartridge
into place. Tighten the set screws to 8 Nm in a cross pattern, then remove the
centering clips before start-up. Failing to remove the centering clips prevents
the seal faces from closing and causes immediate leakage.

## 8. Coupling Alignment

Misalignment is the most common cause of premature bearing and seal failure.
After any work that disturbs the coupling, check the alignment with a dial
indicator or laser tool. The maximum allowable parallel offset is 0.05 mm and
the maximum angular misalignment is 0.05 mm per 100 mm of coupling diameter.
Correct alignment by shimming the motor feet; never move the pump, which is
doweled to the baseplate.

## 9. Troubleshooting

### 9.1 Pump does not deliver liquid
Possible causes: pump not primed, suction valve closed, wrong rotation
direction, or air pocket in the suction line. Prime the pump fully, verify the
suction valve is open, and confirm the motor rotation matches the arrow on the
casing.

### 9.2 Insufficient flow or head
Possible causes: partially closed discharge valve, worn impeller, clogged
strainer, or excessive system resistance. Inspect and clean the suction
strainer, and measure the impeller wear-ring clearance; replace the wear ring
when the clearance exceeds 0.8 mm.

### 9.3 Excessive vibration
Possible causes: coupling misalignment, worn bearings, impeller imbalance due to
deposits, or operation far from the best efficiency point. Check alignment first
(Section 8), then inspect the bearings, and clean the impeller if fouled. Do not
run the pump below 30% of rated flow for extended periods.

### 9.4 Bearing overheating
Possible causes: over-greasing, wrong grease type, misalignment, or excessive
belt tension (not applicable to direct-coupled units). Confirm the grease type
and quantity in Section 5 and verify alignment. Bearing housing temperature
above 80 °C requires immediate shutdown.

### 9.5 Seal leakage
Possible causes: centering clips left in place after installation, worn seal
faces, dry running, or solids in the pumped liquid. If the leak appears
immediately after a seal change, verify the centering clips were removed. A seal
that leaks after long service has worn faces and requires cartridge replacement.

## 11. Storage and Long-Term Shutdown

If the pump is idle for more than one month, rotate the shaft by hand a quarter
turn weekly to prevent bearing brinelling. For shutdowns longer than six months,
drain the casing completely, apply a corrosion inhibitor to the internal
surfaces, and store the unit in a dry environment between 5 °C and 40 °C.
